Single vineyard, Block ix

RESERVE
MMXVIII

Nocturne Reserve comes off two and a half hectares of forty-year-old vines on the cool eastern slope of Block IX, picked in three passes across nine nights in March. Fermented on native yeast in open oak, basket-pressed, then left alone for twenty-two months in French barrique — a third of it new. Unfined, unfiltered, bottled by gravity.

ABV

Alcohol by volume

14.5%

Warm but composed. The fruit carries the alcohol rather than the other way round.

OAK

Months in French barrique

22

One third new Allier, the rest second and third fill. Coopered in Burgundy, toasted long and low.

PH

Total acidity 5.9 g/L

3.62

Cold nights hold the line. This is a wine built to sit in a dark room for a decade.

LONG FINISH

Dense and unhurried. It opens on black fruit — cassis, damson, the skin of a bruised plum — then turns savoury: graphite, dried bay, the inside of a cigar box. The tannin is fine-grained and arrives late, drawing the finish out well past a minute.

STYLE

In the old classification this would sit somewhere between claret and cult — too structured to drink young, too generous to keep waiting on. We make nine hundred cases and we do not make more.

Serve at sixteen degrees, in a glass with room to breathe. Decant an hour ahead. Open the second bottle before you finish the first.

Tasting notes

BLACKCURRANT

Cassis and damson first, dark fruit held together by a fine, graphite edge.

CEDAR — OLIVE

Twenty-two months in Allier oak. Cigar box, dried bay, a thread of sweet spice underneath it all.

VIOLET — IRIS

A floral top note that only shows up twenty minutes into the glass. Wait for it.

The estate

NINE NIGHTS
OF PICKING

Block IX sits four hundred metres up, facing east, on a seam of decomposed granite thin enough to keep the vines honest. Everything is picked at night and in the dark, into small crates, by the same twenty people who have done it for eleven years.

THE ALLOCATION LIST
